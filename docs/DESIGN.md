# SÜLDE — Design Bible

Tone: cold steppe after the sky-banners fall.

## Pillars

1. Every swing costs. Stamina is the real health bar.
2. Read, then commit. Enemy windups are visible.
3. Identity first. Five classes must feel different in three seconds.
4. Death is a sentence. Lose Sülde, walk back, reclaim.
5. The world is one body. Resting respawns lesser dead. Named lords stay down.

## Combat tuning (v0.1)

Light 16 sta · Heavy 32 · Dodge 18 with 280ms i-frames · Sprint 16/s · Flask 42% HP
Regen 28 stamina/s when not attacking, dodging, or sprinting.
